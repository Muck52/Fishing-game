The birds

The gull, the bald eagle, the mallard drake and hen, the song sparrow, and the common loon, who joins them. The three versions

What each one tries

A · Flapping in profile

Every bird seen side-on like the animals on the beach: wings beating up and down beside the body.

  • Matches the land animals' side view
  • The big birds look busy; eagles and gulls mostly soar

B · Spread from below

Both wings outstretched, the far one up behind and the near one down in front, as if passing over you.

  • Big, graceful shapes for the eagle and gull
  • Small birds look stiff; the near wing can read as a leg

C · A mix

The eagle and gull soar spread (B), the mallards and sparrows flap in profile (A), the way each really flies.

  • Each bird moves true to life
  • Two looks in one sky
