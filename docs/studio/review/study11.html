The angler

He was the last character from an art pack: a one-pixel face, his own outline and colours, a stiff stance. He's redrawn here from shapes, like the cast. His head is a little large (study 8's C), he has the cast's eye with a catchlight, and his arms and legs bend at real joints. He gets the same selective edge as the animals and is lit as one mass. The same man is rigged once and dressed three ways.

His poses: idle, fishing, landing, hurt, walking

In the game at midday: standing with the rod on his shoulder, then fishing (the game draws the rod live from his hands so it can bend).

Fishing at dawn, morning, midday, dusk and night. Scroll sideways.
