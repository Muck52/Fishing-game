The fish

All seventeen species are painted by one generator. Next to the redrawn cast they had fallen behind in three ways. The art build snapped their colours to the old pack palette, so they came out muddy (the animals and birds keep their own colours). Their eye was a single dark pixel. At swimming size they showed only one marking each. Each version below builds on the one before it.

The three versions

What each one adds

A · Their own colours, the cast's eye

The same drawing, kept in the colours it was painted in instead of snapped to the pack palette, with the same eye as every other animal: two by two, with a catchlight.

  • True colours: the pumpkinseed's orange, the rainbow's pink band, the brook trout's red belly
  • Shapes unchanged

B · A in the cast's proportions

Deeper, rounder bodies, blunter heads, a thicker tail stalk, a bigger eye on the journal card, like the land animals' C.

  • Friendlier, more of a piece with the cast
  • Slightly less streamlined

C · B in full detail

Every marking drawn even at swimming size (not just the signature one), fin rays, a shimmer of scales on the flank and the lateral line, like the birds' detail pass.

  • The most to look at; each species easier to tell apart
  • Busier at night and at a distance
