Cattails at the shoreline

The cattails were the pack's art, outlined, floating 9 pixels out on the lake's surface band instead of rooted at the water's edge. Here they're tucked in against the beach with their feet in the shallows, and algae floats where they stand. Shown in the game, close up, at five times of day.

The three versions

What each one tries

A · Tucked in

The pack's cattails, moved tight to the shore with their feet in the water, and a mat of green algae floating round them.

  • The least change
  • Still outlined, unlike the rest of the shore now

B · Our stand

Cattails redrawn in our own materials, no outline: fans of arcing leaves, some tips folding over, brown seed heads lit on the sun's side. A dense stand along the shore, the algae mat round their feet, their stems seen through the water with threads of algae on them.

  • Matches the bank and the falls
  • Reads as one stand

C · Mixed shallows

Our cattails in clumps, with bulrush and sedge between them, the algae mat, duckweed scattered on the surface, and a few lily pads out past the stand.

  • The most variety, the most "real shallows"
  • Busier at the water's edge
What stays the same in all three

The rules at work

  1. Rooted at the edge. The plants stand in the shallows just off the beach, a few rows of their stems under the water, not out on the lake.
  2. Through the water. Below the waterline everything is mixed toward the lake's own colours, the way you'd see it through the water.
  3. Algae where it gathers. A soft green mat on the surface round the plants' feet, thick in the middle and breaking into specks at its edges, riding the surface's swell and drifting a little.
  4. Everything moves. Leaves and stalks sway from the tips, the base still; the mat drifts; four frames each.
