# The studio

Where we (you and Claude) work out how Reel It In should look, together,
one step at a time. Claude doesn't remember past sessions; this folder does.
Every session starts by reading it.

## What's here

| File | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| [style-bible.md](style-bible.md) | The rules we've agreed on: palette, light, outlines, scale, materials. A draft until we settle each part. |
| [decisions.md](decisions.md) | Every decision, with the date and why, so nothing gets argued twice. |
| [studies.md](studies.md) | The practice log: each study's subject, references, versions, your verdict, and what we learned. |
| [audit.md](audit.md) | How every piece of art in the game measures up against the rules, and what to redo first. |
| [research/](research/) | The research behind it all, with sources: pixel-art technique for each material, the real north-woods lake, and how other games keep their look cohesive. |
| [audit/](audit/) | The audit's measurements (`audit.json`) and contact sheets. |
| [review/](review/) | The review pages (`research.html`: review 1, published as a page you can open on your phone). |

Tools: `tools/studio/audit.py` re-measures every image in the game.

## How we work

1. **Research** the subject: real photos and facts, and how good pixel art
   handles it.
2. **Study**: Claude makes two or three versions, shown next to the
   reference and the current art on a review page you can open on your
   phone.
3. **You decide**: pick one, or say what feels off. Claude explains why
   each version looks the way it does.
4. **Write it down**: the lesson goes into the style bible, the decision
   into decisions.md, the round into studies.md.
5. **Replace**: once a material is settled, the scene art that uses it is
   redrawn to match, until everything on screen is ours and follows one set
   of rules.

## The plan

1. Audit what we have. *(done: [audit.md](audit.md))*
2. Research. *(done: [research/](research/))*
3. Build the materials library: for each material, a hue-shifted ramp, a
   texture pattern and a test sheet. Wood, water, stone, foliage, bark,
   sand, snow and ice, fur, feathers, fish scales, cloth, metal, rope.
4. Practice studies, one small subject at a time.
5. Replace the pack art, piece by piece.

New game modes are on hold; for now everything goes into the lake we have.
