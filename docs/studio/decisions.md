# Decisions

Newest first. Each one: what we decided, when, and why.

## 2026-10-22: The waterfall is study 3's version A, cut into the bluff

A tiered cascade: the river through a notch in the bluff's top, down three
of the bluff's own ledges, each tier stepping sideways, rock in front of
its edges, wet dark rock and moss beside it, boulders at its foot and an
outflow between stones to the lake.

## 2026-10-22: The far bank is study 2's version C

The birch cove: a pebble beach where the animals come down, white birches
and red pines against dark spruce, and a pink granite bluff with the falls
in a dark cleft, its tea-coloured stream crossing the beach. Replaces the
pack's grassy slope, boulder, big spruces and grey cliff.

## 2026-10-22: The sky is study 1's version B

Low forested hills in haze, the nearest edged in spruce and fir spires, a
banded sky with the sun or moon, and our own rim-lit clouds, drawn for each
time of day. No tall white pines in the treeline: at this size they read as
random mounds. Replaces the pack sky, the snowy peaks and both cloud
styles.

## 2026-10-22: The five calls from review 1

1. **A clear lake.** Green-teal water you can see the bottom and the fish
   through, darkening fast with depth. Watching fish is the game.
2. **Restyle the hut.** Keep our hut, but make it belong up north: gable
   roof, silver-grey weathered wood, barn-red or forest-green trim, the dock
   on galvanized pipe legs, algae-dark where the wood meets the water.
3. **Summer, built for seasons.** The lake stays in summer for now; materials
   and scenes are built so seasons (fall gold, winter white) can follow the
   real calendar later.
4. **Palettes for the time of day.** Instead of one tint over everything,
   each time of day (dawn, day, golden hour, dusk, night) has its own light,
   and every material is drawn in it, so the light changes the colours.
5. **The common loon** joins the wildlife first.

## 2026-10-22: Research before new modes

New game modes are on hold. The work goes into the game we have: research,
then a materials library, practice studies, and replacing the pack art so
everything on screen is ours and follows one set of rules. We do it
together: Claude researches, drafts and builds; you set the direction and
make the calls.

## 2026-10-22: Creatures are finished the same way

Every creature goes through the kit's finishing passes: cleanup, hue-shifted
light and shade, selective outline on the lit top of light parts; tilted
frames turn the RotSprite way. Each has a Pixelorama project; hand edits go
on its touch-ups layer.

## 2026-10-21: One kit for every creature

Olive and all the animals are drawn by our own scripts with one kit: side-on,
lit from above, one palette and outline, real proportions on one scale (half
a pixel to the inch, small animals drawn up to 1.6x), legs through their
joints, real gaits, birds seen from below.

## 2026-10-21: The palette is fixed

`tools/palette.json` holds the master palette; redrawing art never shifts
it. Creature shades are added to it as they're made.
